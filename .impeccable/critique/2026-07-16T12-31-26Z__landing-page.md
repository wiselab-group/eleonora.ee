## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Yes, this reads as AI-made. Three reflex patterns stack: Playfair Display italic carrying 100% of the personality load; the editorial-typographic lane executed to spec (serif + italic + tracked kickers + warm monochrome + rounded corners + soft shadows); the tiny uppercase tracked kicker appearing on all 5 below-the-fold sections via one shared component, byte-for-byte identical every time.

Deterministic scan (detect.mjs): 2 advisory findings — 13px font-size off the DESIGN.md type ramp (Header.tsx:22, confirmed real), undocumented #fff in globals.css:64 (plausible false positive).

Assessment B's contrast measurements (accent button 2.73:1, kickers 2.44:1) independently corroborate Assessment A's qualitative read, and B's discovery of the empty About-section div (no `<img>` at all) corroborates A's read that the "real person" promise breaks exactly where PRODUCT.md says it should land hardest.

## Overall Impression

Good bones — real hero photography, honest specific service copy, coherent token system, zero console errors — but the execution defaults to the most saturated brand-register template available instead of finding something specific to Eleonora, and two implementation gaps (broken mobile header CTA, unphotographed About section) undercut the site's core purpose.

## What's Working

1. The hero photo — candid, well color-graded, arch-shaped crop is a real point of specificity.
2. Services section copy — six services with real prices/durations/deliverables, strongly on-brief with "specificity over superlatives."
3. Token discipline — clean architecture, no console errors, mostly consistent `var(--color-*)` usage.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Mobile header CTA clipped off-screen at 375/390/414px** — Header.tsx; overflow-x:hidden clips instead of the header adapting. Breaks "low-friction contact everywhere" at the top of the page for majority mobile traffic. Suggested: /impeccable adapt

**[P0] About section portrait is a literal empty box, no `<img>` at all** — About.tsx lines 20-23; contradicts PRODUCT.md naming About as a belief-ladder credibility carrier and the "practice what's taught" principle. Suggested: direct fix (asset + wiring)

**[P1] Accent-button text contrast fails WCAG AA — measured 2.73:1, needs 4.5:1** — white text on #c98e84 accent, DESIGN.md's own "highest-intent CTA" treatment; kickers/numerals also fail at 2.4-2.8:1. PRODUCT.md commits to 4.5:1. Suggested: /impeccable audit then /impeccable colorize

**[P1] All 8 CTAs link to the same undifferentiated Telegram chat, no pre-filled context** — PRODUCT.md's stated success metric requires context-carrying CTAs; six differentiated services all drop into one blank DM. Suggested: /impeccable clarify or direct fix

**[P2] Zero compositional variation across 5 consecutive sections** — Mission/About/Services/Feed/Contact all use identical kicker+Playfair-heading formula via SectionKicker.tsx. Most fixable lever on the AI-slop verdict. Suggested: /impeccable typeset or /impeccable layout

## Minor Observations

- Header wordmark and Hero H1 both render "Eleonora Kupczyk" in italic Playfair — redundant branding beat.
- ServiceRow.tsx uses three near-identical hardcoded terracotta hex values not matching documented tokens (CLAUDE.md "no raw hex colors" violation).
- Feed grid's 6 tiles include one duplicate image (t0_0 appears twice in feedImages).
- No target="_blank" on external links — every CTA navigates fully away with no easy way back.
- Detector's #fff flag in globals.css:64 is likely a legitimate utility value, not real drift.

## Questions to Consider

- If Playfair italic and the tracked kicker were removed entirely, what would this brand's typography have to become to feel like one specific person's space?
- Is the Restrained color strategy reading as confidence, or as safety, for a "warm, confident, approachable" personal brand?
- Why do six differentiated services all funnel into the same blank chat despite it being a near-zero-cost fix?
